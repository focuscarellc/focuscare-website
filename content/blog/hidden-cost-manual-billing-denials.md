## The Silent Leak in Your Revenue

As a care provider, you know that billing is the lifeblood of your organization. Yet, so many providers still rely on manual processes that are prone to error, delay, and ultimately, denial.

It's not just about the hours lost to data entry. It's about the revenue that never makes it to your bank account.

## The Denial Trap

Retro-authorizations. Missing signatures. Incorrect service codes.

These small mistakes compound into significant financial losses.

When billing and service delivery are disconnected, errors happen. A staff member forgets to clock out, or a code is mistyped.

### The Problem with Manual

* **Human Error:** Typos and overlooked fields are inevitable.
* **Delayed Payments:** Investigating denials can take weeks or months.
* **Lost Time:** Staff spend valuable hours correcting mistakes instead of providing care.

## Bridging the Gap: Automated Workflows

We help providers automate the critical flow between EVV (Electronic Visit Verification) systems like Sandata and billing software like AxisCare or Therap.

By integrating these systems, we eliminate the manual steps where errors occur.

### The Power of Automation

* **Seamless Data Transfer:** Eliminate manual data entry between systems.
* **Instant Validation:** Flag potential billing errors before submission.
* **Faster Reimbursements:** Get paid accurately and on time.
